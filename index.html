Guideline & sources

When SUCCEED applies

The guideline covers studies that evaluate deliberate expansion of an evidence-informed health intervention beyond its original setting, across organisations, jurisdictions, populations or delivery systems. Prior evidence or demonstrated promise and an understanding of potential harms are expected. The report describes both the intervention and its scaling strategy. The guideline applies across study designs, sample sizes and sectors.

The paper excludes single-setting implementation without broader expansion, early development or initial efficacy or feasibility testing, and effectiveness-only studies that omit evaluation of scaling processes, scalability, reach or system-level changes. See “When SUCCEED can be used” in the article for the full criteria.
